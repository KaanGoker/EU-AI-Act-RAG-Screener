Before you start

A prototype for orientation, not legal advice

  • A draft screening, not legal advice. Results can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Check them against the official text and a qualified lawyer before you rely on them.
  • 18 or older. You must be 18 or older. The Gemini API terms do not allow use by anyone under 18.
  • No personal or confidential data. Do not paste names, emails, IDs, CVs, health data, contracts or internal company details.

  • Nothing is stored by this app. It stores and logs nothing you type and saves no results. Your acceptance and your theme are kept in this browser only.
