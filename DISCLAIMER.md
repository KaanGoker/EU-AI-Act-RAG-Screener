# Disclaimer

Read this before you use the EU AI Act Screener. By clicking "I am 18 or older and I agree" in the app, you confirm that you have read and accepted it.

## Not legal advice

- The EU AI Act Screener is a prototype for orientation. It gives a draft screening of a system description, not a legal determination, and it does not replace advice from a qualified lawyer.
- The result can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. That includes the class: a language model reads the description, and it can misread it or be too sure of itself.
- Do not use the result as the only basis for a compliance decision, an audit or a legal conclusion.

## What the tool checks

- The six checks (scope, prohibited practices, high-risk, the Article 6(3) exemption, transparency duties, general-purpose AI), their order and the names of the classes (such as "limited risk" and "minimal risk") are this tool's simplification. The Act does not prescribe such a checklist.
- A quote marked as verified was found in the provision the result cites, exactly, after folding whitespace, quote marks, hyphens and letter case. That shows the words are in the text. It does not show that the conclusion drawn from them is right.
- A quote that was not found stays on the page with a flag.
- The obligations and the dates come from data files written from the text of the Act and checked as of 11 November 2026. The law, its dates and the guidance around it can change after that.
- Commission guidelines mentioned in the sources are not binding. The tool does not cover national law.

## Which text is quoted

- Articles and annexes: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 in the EUR-Lex consolidated version of 27 July 2026, which includes the amendments of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI).
- Recitals: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as published in the Official Journal of 12 July 2024. The consolidated version does not include them.
- A consolidated text is a documentation tool with no legal effect. The authentic texts are the ones published in the Official Journal of the European Union: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. Check any result against them.
- The app quotes its own parsed copy of these texts, in which page headers, footnotes and consolidation markers are removed and long dashes are written as hyphens.
